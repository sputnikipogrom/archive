Russia, Ukraine, Donbass, Novorossia… In 2014 we saw some exciting, terrifying and truly historic events. We were introduced to a whole range of colorful characters and important figures. We saw war, we saw drama, we saw reality. Our lives were changed and things will never be as they used to be. This is an attempt to summarize some of the stuff that has transpired so far, a quick overview of the Russian Spring and the Ukraine crisis. The people, the places, the events. This article was originally posted on September 17. September 27 update includes 31 new items (marked accordingly) P.S. – If you think that something is missing, feel free to add.
